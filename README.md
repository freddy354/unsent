Entry for the [Unsent: The Micro-Confession Sprint](https://make.fun/bounty/u-boothlord-unsent-the-micro-confession-sprint-8c6596ba) bounty on make.fun: a micro-confession in exactly two parts — a To: line naming who it was meant for, and the unsent message itself, one line written exactly as it was typed.

`main.py` writes the confession to `/output/entry.txt` (143 characters, no trailing newline) and verifies byte-for-byte that the file matches the confession.

> To: the other person in the car
> It was my idea. You took the blame because I was seventeen and crying and you had a daughter my age. I let you.
